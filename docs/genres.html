Genre trends on the Billboard Hot 100

Pick a genre to see its Hot 100 chart appearances per year, from 1970. Genre is assigned at the artist level from MusicBrainz tags (top-tag-wins); Other/Unknown is a coverage residual (unmatched artists + the long tail of artists with < 20 Hot 100 appearances), not a musical category. The Hot 100 ranks a blended metric (sales + airplay + streaming); streaming entered the formula in 2013, so cross-era comparisons carry that caveat.
